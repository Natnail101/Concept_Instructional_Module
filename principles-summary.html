How I Used the Multimedia and Contiguity Principles

Back to Module

This programmed tutorial teaches a use-level concept: deciding whether an image is accessible based on whether the alt text communicates purpose. I focused more on instructional design and making the system work than trying to make this a “multimedia production.”

Multimedia principle: I used text plus visual support so learners are not learning from words alone. Learners see the rule, then apply it immediately with examples where the alt text is shown on the same screen. After each response, learners can open “Show me why” to see a breakdown of what the alt text communicates.

Contiguity principle: I kept related information close together to reduce split attention. The prompt and the alt text appear together, and feedback appears immediately after the learner responds. The “Show me why” explanation stays tied to the exact item the learner answered.

The module uses text and graphics (layout, grouping, highlighted feedback). Audio and video were not required.
